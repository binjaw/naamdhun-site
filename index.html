Naamdhun

नामधुन · Naam Jap Counter

A free, offline naam-jap counter for Android. Tap the gold button, hear the naam, count your malas of 108, keep a daily target, streaks and long-term sankalps. Fifteen deity groups with 86 japas, and your own japas in your own voice.

  • Count by voice: chant aloud and it counts each naam, offline, after you teach it your voice once
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission
  • Everything stays on your phone
  • Real paintings as backgrounds, all public domain
